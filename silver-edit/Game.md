# The Silver Edit

Blackwood, Vermont. A town caught in a permanent autumn. Elena Vance, forty-two, once second-in-command of a Pacific Northwest coven, has burnt out her own magic to escape Malphas, and taken a job as senior editor at *Vellum & Vine*.

**This slice:** Elena's first day. Talk to Arthur Thorne, Miles, and Chloe at the office, go home, and face Malphas in a dream.
